# Nizamlar tee model provenance

`nizamlar-tee.glb` is a new export of the DiMaac free Clothes Rack tee drape and procedural cloth texture, without hanger or hook. Export recipe: `scripts/export-shirt-entry.ts` and `scripts/export-shirt.cjs`. Coordinates remain metres; the model is centred for all three experiences.

Source: https://ui.dimaac.com/r/clothes-rack.json
Author: DiMaac (HankTheTank), https://dimaac.com
Commercial/client-project permission: https://ui.dimaac.com/docs#licence (checked 2026-11-03). Free components may be used in personal and commercial/client projects; redistribution as a component library/template pack/UI kit is prohibited. No reference demo photographs or external texture scans are included.

The older `tshirt.glb` was already present before this audit and has no recorded licence. It is retained for rollback but is no longer referenced by the application. No VeloriQ or TDesigner model/code was copied.
